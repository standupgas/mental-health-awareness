Small steps. Real support.

Take Action

You do not have to handle bullying or hard feelings alone. Here are safe, useful ways to help.

Help Prevent Bullying

  • Learn to notice different forms of bullying.
  • Tell a trusted adult or school staff member what happened.
  • Support someone being targeted without putting yourself in danger.
  • Practice kindness and respect.
  • Do not share harmful online content. Save evidence and report it.

Care for Your Mental Health

  • Notice your feelings and try putting them into words.
  • Take healthy breaks for rest and activities you enjoy.
  • Keep in touch with people who treat you with care.
  • Do not judge or compare another person's experience.
  • Reach out to a trusted adult or trained professional.

You deserve to be heard

Find Someone Who Can Help.

If you or someone else is in urgent danger, call local emergency services or tell a trusted adult. This website cannot provide counseling or emergency help.

01

At School

Talk with your guidance counsellor, class adviser, trusted teacher, school nurse, or school child-protection worker.

02

At Home or in Your Community

Reach out to a parent, guardian, relative, coach, or another responsible adult who will listen.

03

A trained mental health professional can listen and explain your support options.
