Care for your whole self

Mental Health Matters

Mental health is part of being healthy. It can change, and help is available.

What is mental health?

Mental health affects our feelings, thoughts, relationships, and daily life.

Why mental health matters

Feeling supported can help us learn, make friends, and handle hard situations. Everyone has mental health, and everyone's experience is different.

Common myths

Mental health problems are not a person's fault. They can look different for everyone, so we should not judge by appearance.

Getting support

You do not need to wait until things feel too hard. A trusted adult or trained professional can listen and help.

Name it to address it

Understanding Bullying

Bullying is repeated behavior where one person has, or seems to have, more power than another.

01

Verbal

Repeated insults, threats, name-calling, or mean comments.

02

Physical

Hitting, pushing, damaging belongings, or other unwanted physical acts.

03

Social

Leaving someone out on purpose, embarrassing them, or spreading rumours to hurt them.

04

Cyberbullying

Using digital spaces to threaten, harass, embarrass, or repeatedly target someone.

!

Bullying can affect different parts of life.

It may affect confidence, feelings, friendships, and a student's comfort at school. Its effect is different for each person. It is never the target's fault.
